<h3 align="center">Scroll into the picture.</h3>

Divetrope is an open-source web component for endless-zoom stories. Each
scene hides the next one in its centre, and the last one hides the first.
Readers scroll, drag or press Play to dive through them.

- [**divetrope/divetrope**](https://github.com/divetrope/divetrope): the
  `<divetrope-player>` element, the build script and the tests. MIT licensed.
- [**The landing page**](https://divetrope.github.io/divetrope/) is itself a
  dive: from Earth down into a glass of champagne, out past a galaxy and
  home again.
- [**The embed demo**](https://divetrope.github.io/divetrope/demo.html) shows
  the three ways to put one on a page.
- [**A one-minute commercial**](https://edu.yepgent.com/watch/) for Yep Edu
  ends on a call to action.
